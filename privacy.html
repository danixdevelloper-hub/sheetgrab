SheetGrab — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025.

Summary

SheetGrab does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. All processing happens locally in your browser.

What SheetGrab accesses

When you click the SheetGrab icon or the Pick button, the extension reads the tables and lists on the current tab (including iframes and open shadow roots) so it can show them and export them. This access is granted only when you use the extension (activeTab and scripting permissions). It does not run in the background and does not read pages you have not interacted with.

What is stored

SheetGrab saves your preferences (theme, CSV delimiter, export options, and a local usage counter used to decide when to show the review prompt) in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device. Removing the extension deletes it.

What is sent over the network

Nothing. SheetGrab makes no network requests for analytics, tracking, or data collection. The spreadsheet library used for Excel export is bundled inside the extension.

Third parties

SheetGrab uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children

SheetGrab does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
